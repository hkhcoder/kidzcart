How it works

Four quick steps — you’ve got this!

  1. 1
    Look around Open Books, Cart, or Donate from the top menu and see what’s there.
  2. 2
    Fill your cart Tap “Add to cart” on things your family loves.
  3. 3
    Check out Review your list with a grown-up and finish your order (it’s a pretend checkout for learning!).
  4. 4
    Donate & cheer Share items you’re done with — you might even earn a thank-you surprise.
🛡️

Grown-ups & safety

KidzCart is built for learning. Use a strong password with a parent or guardian, and ask for help if something looks odd. Shopping here is a team adventure — grown-ups make the big decisions; kids bring the curiosity and kindness.
